Medical And Safety Notice

Focusidea is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, prescriptions, dosage instructions, emergency decisions, or medical acts. In an emergency, contact local emergency services immediately. For health decisions, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Weight Forecast is a reference estimate based on three consecutive days of meal and exercise records and the current weight entered in the user's profile. It is not medical judgment, weight-loss instruction, or treatment guidance.

External AI And Provider Costs

Some Focusidea features may connect to external AI providers selected by the user. Provider accounts, subscription plans, token purchases, and external fees are managed by those providers or stores, not by Focusidea support.

Focusidea is a monthly subscription app priced at USD 6.99 per month. External AI provider fees are billed to you directly by those providers, are separate from, and are not marked up by, the Focusidea subscription.

Permissions

Focusidea may request permissions for camera, photos, microphone, speech recognition, location, notifications, and Face ID/device authentication. Permissions can be changed in the iPhone Settings app.
