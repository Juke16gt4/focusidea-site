Medical And Safety Notice

Focusidea is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, prescriptions, dosage instructions, emergency decisions, or medical acts. In an emergency, contact local emergency services immediately. For health decisions, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Weight Forecast is a reference estimate based on three consecutive days of meal and exercise records and the current weight entered in the user's profile. It is not medical judgment, weight-loss instruction, or treatment guidance.

AI Features And Their Cost

AI features are provided as part of the app. You are never asked to create an account with an AI provider, and you never enter an API key. On supported devices the reply is produced entirely on your device; otherwise the request goes to a gateway operated by us, which performs the work. All credentials are held on our server only.

The Focusidea subscription is the only charge. No AI provider bills you, because you hold no account with any of them. See the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use for details.

If an AI feature is temporarily unavailable, it is usually a maintenance window or an outage at a model provider. Please try again after a short while. There is nothing for you to configure.

Permissions

Focusidea may request permissions for camera, photos, microphone, speech recognition, location, notifications, and Face ID/device authentication. Permissions can be changed in the iPhone Settings app.

Die Abrechnung erfolgt ausschließlich über das Focusidea-Abonnement. Kein KI-Anbieter stellt Ihnen eine Rechnung, da Sie bei keinem von ihnen ein Konto besitzen. Einzelheiten finden Sie in der Datenschutzerklärung und den Nutzungsbedingungen.

Ist eine KI-Funktion vorübergehend nicht verfügbar, handelt es sich in der Regel um ein Wartungsfenster oder eine Störung bei einem Modellanbieter. Bitte versuchen Sie es nach kurzer Zeit erneut. Es gibt nichts, was Sie konfigurieren müssten.
